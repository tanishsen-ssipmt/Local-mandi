📌 Quality Rating कैसे काम करता है?

About Local Mandi

Local Mandi is a trusted B2B platform that connects Farmers directly with verified local Mandi buyers (millers, traders, processors).

We help farmers get fair local prices, discover reliable nearby buyers with ratings & payment history, and reduce middlemen dependency.

How it works

1

Select crop & location

Farmer chooses the crop and sees live local prices by quality grade.

2

See verified buyers

List of nearby KYC-verified buyers with ratings, payment reliability and distance.

3

Deal with trust

Contact buyer, sell at fair price. If any issue — use complaint & unpaid support.

Key Features

  • 💰 Live local prices with quality-grade rates (A / B / C)
  • ✅ Verified Mandi buyers with KYC & ratings
  • 📊 Payment history & reliability score
  • 📈 3–6 month demand forecast
  • 🛡️ Complaint & unpaid amount support
  • 🗣️ Simple Hindi + local language interface
